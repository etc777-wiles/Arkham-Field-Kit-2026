## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **The Sinking City 2**. This trainer provides customizable enhancements for health, ammunition, reloads, inventory resources, enemy encounters, and game speed during single-player exploration and combat.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to reduce survival pressure, explore Arkham freely, test weapons and encounters, or revisit investigations without strict resource limitations.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## ⚡ Features (v1.0)

### 🎯 Gameplay

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents the player character from losing health during exploration and combat.
- **Unlimited Inventory Resources** — Prevents selected supplies and usable resources from being depleted.
- **Game Speed Control** — Adjusts the overall game speed for slower investigation or faster traversal.

### ⚔️ Combat

- **Unlimited Ammunition** — Keeps firearm ammunition available while fighting hostile creatures.
- **No Reload** — Removes the need to perform manual weapon reloads.
- **One-Hit Kills** — Allows attacks to defeat standard enemies with a single successful hit.
- **Freeze Enemies** — Stops hostile creatures from moving while leaving the player free to explore or attack.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Explore Arkham on foot and by skiff, search flooded districts, and use unlocked shortcuts to revisit areas efficiently. Enable Game Speed Control when you want to slow down tense traversal or move through previously explored locations more quickly.

### Tools & Inventory

The game emphasizes careful inventory management, ammunition conservation, supplies, and upgrades. Enable Unlimited Inventory Resources before entering dangerous areas or testing different equipment choices. Turn the option off when you want the intended survival-horror balance.

### World & Environment

Investigate Arkham by examining ritual residues, clues, journals, masks, and environmental details. Optional investigations can provide safer routes, supplies, weapon upgrades, and other advantages without blocking the main story.

### Combat & Weapons

The sequel places greater emphasis on survival-horror combat against threats such as the Slither, Deep Ones, and other eldritch creatures. Use Infinite Health, Unlimited Ammunition, No Reload, One-Hit Kills, or Freeze Enemies independently to customize the difficulty of each encounter.
